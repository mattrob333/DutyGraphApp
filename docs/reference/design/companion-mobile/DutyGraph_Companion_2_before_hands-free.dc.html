Design notes

Companion 2.0 keeps v1's three primitives and builds around them: one voice conversation (Pulse, Discovery, Clarification, Something changed), one "Here's what we understood" review (also used for meeting wrap-up), one consent / type-instead layer.

Violet = DutyGraph speaking or unsent interpretation. Sage = confirmed, sent, or time returned. Amber = due. Red = error or overdue.

Suggested path: Pulse → Voice → answer 4 questions → submit → team status fills in → "no need to join" arrives. Then Meetings → 2:00 → join → Meeting ended → confirm outcome → it lands in Work.

All content fictional; recording, storage and sync are simulated.
