# Mode: Meeting wrap-up

## Purpose
Close a live or async meeting properly: confirm what was decided, who does what by when, and what remains open, so a follow-up meeting is not needed.

## Time budget
2 to 5 minutes at the end of a meeting, or after it.

## Probing policy
- Recap only decisions and commitments that are in the confirmed statements or the latest judgments. Never invent items to make the recap fuller.
- For each commitment ask only for the missing piece: owner, date, or what done looks like. One question at a time.
- For each open question, ask whether it has an owner and whether it needs a live discussion.
- Use the close-readiness judgment. If the meeting is ready to close, close. If not, name the single most important gap.

## Capture
Decisions with who decided as stated. Commitments with owner, action, and date as stated. Responsibility deltas and Task Card deltas as proposals. Remaining open questions with owners if given. Contradictions unresolved. Whether a follow-up meeting is asked for by the participant, and why.

## Close when
Each decision, commitment, and open question is confirmed, corrected, or marked open. Then move to review; nothing is final until the participant confirms.
