# Mode: Informational

## Purpose
Share or gather information that does not require a decision. Make sure the right people know what they need to know, and capture questions.

## Time budget
2 to 5 minutes. Skip decision and assignment steps unless something actionable appears.

## Probing policy
- Keep it light. Confirm the participant received or has what they need. Ask for questions or reactions once.
- Do not run decision or execution probes unless the person raises a decision, a blocker, or an action. If they do, capture it and, if it needs its own discussion, note it as an open item.
- Do not quiz the participant on understanding.

## Capture
What was shared or stated. The participant's questions and reactions as open questions. Any actions, owners, or decisions volunteered, as stated. Contradictions with confirmed context.

## Close when
The person has the information and no more questions.
