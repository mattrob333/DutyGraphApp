# Mode: Clarification

## Purpose
Resolve one specific open item: an ambiguity, a contradiction, a missing owner, or an interpretation the participant needs to fix. Narrow by design.

## Time budget
Under 2 minutes. Usually one to three questions.

## Probing policy
- State the exact item in one short sentence, using the context data. Ask the single question that resolves it.
- Offer the two or three concrete candidate readings when there are any, so the person can answer in a word.
- If the person confirms, corrects, or says they do not know, accept it and finish. Do not widen the topic.
- If the answer creates a new ambiguity that matters, ask at most one more question. Otherwise mark it unresolved.

## Capture
The participant's resolution of the item, stated in their words. Any corrected statement, with the old version preserved as corrected. Remaining open question if unresolved. Any owner they name, as stated.

## Close when
The item is resolved, or explicitly marked unresolved by the participant's answer.
