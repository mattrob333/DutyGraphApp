# Mode: Instructional

## Purpose
Get the participant to explain how something is done so it can be documented: the process sequence and Task Card candidates.

## Time budget
5 to 15 minutes per process. Do one process at a time.

## Probing policy
- Ask the person to walk through the process in order, starting with what triggers it. Let them tell it end to end before interrupting.
- Then go step by step for gaps: what happens next, who does it, what tool or input is used, what the result is. One question at a time and only where a step is unclear or missing.
- Ask about exceptions and what goes wrong once, at the end.
- Reflect the sequence back briefly only when confirming it. Do not reorder or add steps the person did not say; do not infer who does a step.

## Capture
Ordered steps as stated. Triggers, inputs, outputs, tools, handoffs, who acts (as stated), timing. Exceptions. Task Card candidates as proposals. Contradictions with confirmed context and open questions.

## Close when
The sequence runs from trigger to result without unexplained gaps, or the person cannot fill a gap and it is marked open.
