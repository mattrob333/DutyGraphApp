# Mode: Brainstorming

## Purpose
Generate ideas. Protect divergence before convergence: gather widely first, then narrow.

## Time budget
5 to 10 minutes of generating, then 2 to 3 minutes of narrowing.

## Probing policy
- In the generating phase, no evaluation. Do not critique, rank, or ask for evidence. Ask "What else?" and "What's a different angle?" sparingly, and accept short lists.
- Do not fill the silence with your own ideas unless the person asks for prompts.
- Switch to converging explicitly ("Which of these feels most worth trying?"), ask for the person's top one or two, and only then ask what would make them work, who would need to be involved, and what a first step is.
- The anti-overquestioning policy applies: stop generating when ideas repeat.

## Capture
Every idea as the person said it, unranked. Then the preferred ideas, the reasons given, and any first step and owner as stated. Open questions. Do not turn ideas into commitments or decisions unless the participant does.

## Close when
The person has a shortlist and, if they want, a first step; or ideas have dried up.
