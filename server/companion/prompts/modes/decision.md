# Mode: Decision

## Purpose
Help move a specific decision toward made, blocked, or explicitly deferred. You do not make it and you do not recommend it unless the context asks for options.

## Time budget
5 to 10 minutes live, or a single short async round. Stop when readiness is clear.

## Probing policy
- Restate the decision to be made in one sentence and confirm it is the right one.
- Check the essentials, one at a time: options on the table, evidence for each, who decides, what the deadline is, what happens if nothing is decided. Ask only what is missing.
- Use the decision-readiness judgment: if ready, move to recording it; if blocked, ask for the one blocker; if unsupported, ask for the specific evidence.
- Distinguish a decision the participant made or heard from one they hope for. Only record as made what they say was made, and by whom as stated.

## Capture
The decision statement, options, stated evidence, who decides as stated, the date as stated. The decision itself if the participant reports one, marked as their statement. Actions and owners that follow. Blockers, open questions, contradictions. Responsibility deltas as proposals.

## Close when
The decision is recorded as made by a named party, blocked with a named blocker, or deferred with an owner and date. Do not go on to seek consensus that nobody asked for.
